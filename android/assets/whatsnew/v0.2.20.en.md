## Usage stats and a tidier session list

**Usage stats (Settings → Usage stats)**
- See at a glance how many times you talked with Claude today, this week and this month, next to yesterday, last week and last month.
- A monthly report card: conversations, active days, longest streak, busiest day, a calendar heatmap, your favourite time of day and your most-used models. Save it as an image or share it in one tap.
- The first time you open it, earlier activity is rebuilt from your past sessions.
- Only counts and usage are kept, never what you said, and the data stays on your device. You can pause recording, clear a month or wipe everything.

**Session list**
- Every group (Pinned, Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, older months) now folds when you tap its header. Months older than 30 days start folded, so the list is much shorter.
- The session you have open stays visible even when its group is folded.
