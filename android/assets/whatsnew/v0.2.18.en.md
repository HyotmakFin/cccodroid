## Steadier Claude Code downloads

- The official manifest is now also mirrored on a direct line (dl.cccodroid.net) that doesn't go through Cloudflare, so networks that can't reach cccodroid.net can still get the manifest with Anthropic's signature.
- Small files such as the version number and the manifest are retried once on failure.
- If every source fails, the app waits 15 seconds and tries another round, which rides out short network drops. Whatever was already downloaded is kept and resumed.
