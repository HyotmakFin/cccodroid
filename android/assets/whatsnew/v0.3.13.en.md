This release rebuilds the interface top to bottom: the sidebar, the full-page screens, the new-session screen and the fonts all look different, and there are two new features, projects and scheduled tasks. There's a lot in it; here are the parts that matter.

## A new sidebar

- From top to bottom: New chat, navigation, recent conversations, and your account at the bottom. You choose which navigation items stay pinned; the rest live under "More".
- Settings, Extensions, Capabilities, Usage and Help now open as full pages in the main area, with a back button. On wide screens they leave the sidebar and the right-hand panes alone, and the six top-bar buttons are unchanged.
- The sidebar can be resized by dragging its edge, both when it's docked in landscape and when it slides out in portrait; double-tap to reset. In portrait it now opens wider by default.
- The account row shows just your avatar, nickname and the current provider (account login, DeepSeek, Kimi…) instead of a pile of model names and addresses.

## Avatar and nickname

Set them under Settings → General, or jump there from the account menu. Without a nickname, the part of your login email before the @ is used. Exported monthly usage reports carry your avatar and nickname in the top-right corner; the report inside the app doesn't show them.

## Projects

- A project is a working directory plus its CLAUDE.md and default model, thinking effort and permissions.
- Sessions are filed under a project by their working directory, and you can move them in by hand. When starting a new chat you can pick a project first.

## Scheduled tasks

- Have Claude do something on a schedule: daily, weekly, monthly, every few hours, once, or with a cron expression.
- Limit it to read-only, to changing this project only, or to tools you pick. You can also set conditions, a per-run cost and time cap, a daily budget, what to do about missed or failed runs, and whether to notify only when it finds something.
- Nobody is watching while it runs, so anything that would need your confirmation is refused; it never clicks "Allow" for you.

## The new-session screen

- In the middle are our own line-art logo (traced from the app icon, colored by your theme) and a greeting that depends on the time of day, the day of the week and your battery: running low, or charging late at night, gets you a different line.
- The input box sits in the center, with project, model, thinking effort and permissions right below it, so you can set them before the session starts.

## Fonts

- The app now ships its own fonts: Inter and Source Han Sans for the interface, Spectral and Source Han Serif for replies, so your phone's system font no longer changes how it looks.
- Text is a little smaller overall, and bold weights were retuned so emphasis doesn't look heavy.
- Settings let you change the interface font, the reply font, and the body size (small, medium, large).

## Release notes

A new Release notes page shows the notes for every release from GitHub, in Chinese if the interface is Chinese and in English otherwise. Like Help, it has search and a table of contents, and only the releases near what's on screen are rendered, so scrolling through dozens of them stays smooth. Open it from the account menu, the sidebar navigation, Settings → Update, or the button at the bottom of the what's-new card.

## Also new

- **Delete messages**: questions and replies each have a delete button; a reply goes together with its tool calls, and you can undo.
- **Enter can insert a newline**: under Settings → Enter key, choose "New line" and send with Ctrl + Enter or the send button. Many phone keyboards have no Shift key; this came from a user request.
- **The + panel** is now four tiles plus common actions, shown as a floating card on wide screens, with the paperclip folded into it.
- **Usage** has its own page, with subscription quota in large numbers that refresh after each turn and when you come back to the app.

## Fixes

- **The app didn't come back after an automatic update**: the silent update installed fine but the window never reopened, because the system drops launch requests while it is still scanning a freshly installed app. The app now checks that it really is in the foreground and relaunches up to three times, then posts a notification if that still fails.
- **Sharing text in from another app showed an error**: the text was inserted, but the top bar reported "t is not a function".
- **The top bar said only "Script error."**: it now names the entry point and the reason, so a report can be traced. See Known issues.
- **The slash-command list, the permission menu and the model panel** are no longer half hidden behind the browser or terminal pane on the right, and the slash list stays attached to the input box as it grows or the keyboard opens.
- The "中文 / back to Chinese" button that popped up after errors in non-Chinese interfaces is gone.
- The remaining emoji in the interface were replaced with line icons.
