This release starts foldable support, still experimental. It also fixes a first-install problem.

## Foldable layout (experimental)

- **Split along the fold when unfolded**: on book-style and wide foldables, the sidebar sits on the left page and the chat on the right. Open files, the viewer or the terminal and the sidebar steps aside: the pane takes one page, the chat the other, and the divider lands right on the fold.
- **One job per panel on a tri-fold**: fully unfolded, the Samsung tri-fold shows the sidebar on the left panel, the chat in the middle and the side pane on the right, with each divider on a fold.
- **Nothing on the fold when half-open**: holding a book-style foldable half-open, dialogs and bottom sheets appear only on the chat page.
- **Input on the lower half when standing up**: with a flip phone, or a foldable turned sideways, standing half-open, the chat stays on top and the input box starts below the fold, where the keyboard opens too.
- **Folded works as before**: the cover screen uses the usual phone portrait and landscape layouts.
- **Can be turned off** in Settings → Appearance → "Foldable layout (experimental)". The option only shows up on foldables.

We don't have a foldable, so all of this was checked on the Android emulator using the specs of the Pixel 9 Pro Fold, a 6.7" flip, a wide foldable and the Samsung tri-fold. If you use a foldable, screenshots and reports in #19 are very welcome.

## Fixes

- **First-run setup didn't run on fresh installs of the 36 build**: setup for openssh, npm, pip and others in the toolkit was skipped, and the install screen showed a `CANNOT LINK … exec-shim-bionic.so` line. The order is fixed now, and installs already affected re-run it automatically the first time you open the app after updating.
