This release is mostly security hardening. It also fixes several actions on very large conversations and how sub-agent status is shown.

## Security

- Fixed several security issues.
- Files shared in from other apps are now refused if they point into cccodroid's own private folder.
- Session packages are now imported one at a time.

## Fixes

- Very large conversations (hundreds of MB, even over 1 GB) can now be exported, imported, forked, rewound, and have messages deleted. Above roughly 500 MB these used to fail outright.
- After you stop a reply partway and come back to the conversation, sub-agents and the Agent card no longer keep showing as running.
