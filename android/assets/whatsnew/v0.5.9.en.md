This release fixes a few everyday annoyances: each conversation keeps its own draft, usage stats keep up with what you actually use, and the help page table of contents jumps where you tap.

## Each conversation keeps its own draft

- Text you typed but didn't send used to follow you into another conversation. Drafts and attachments are now kept per conversation, and they're still there when you switch back or reopen the app.

## Usage stats keep up

- Usage used to be recorded only when a turn finished, so a long turn showed nothing until the end, and a turn you stopped wasn't counted at all. It's now recorded as it happens, and the usage page refreshes while it's open.
- Tokens spent by subagents weren't counted before, so the numbers could be far too low if you use subagents a lot. They're counted now.
- This applies to usage recorded from this version on; earlier numbers don't change. Claude runs started directly in the terminal aren't included.

## Help and changelog table of contents

- On narrow screens, tapping a tab at the top sometimes stopped scrolling halfway, and tapping one of the last sections highlighted a different one. It now goes to, and highlights, the section you tap.

## Other

- In Russian, the "Reset" button in settings could be squeezed into a vertical column at some widths. Fixed.
- The viewer frees large files when closed, and the app no longer polls for status while in the background.
- The message box and several dialogs now have names that screen readers can read.
