## Fix: a dismissed task list came back after reopening the app

Once a task list was finished, or a goal was reached or dropped, tapping ✕ on its strip above the conversation hid it only until you left the app. That choice is now remembered per conversation, so it stays hidden after a restart. If the list gets new tasks, it shows up again as usual.

Thanks to the group member who reported it.

## Architecture change: the gateway is now split into modules

The part of the app that runs Claude and handles conversations and files (the gateway) used to be one file of more than seven thousand lines, where any change meant a long search and could easily break something elsewhere. It is now split by feature into some thirty smaller modules: conversations, files, git, terminal, components, queueing, on-device models and so on.

Nothing changes in how the app works, and before release we compared the old and new gateways side by side, endpoint by endpoint. Still, this touches a lot of code, and some corner may have slipped through. If something that used to work now misbehaves (an error, a page that won't open, a button that does nothing), please tell us soon: "Report on GitHub" in Settings fills in your device and version for you, or post in the QQ group 1062538743. The sooner we hear, the sooner it's fixed.
