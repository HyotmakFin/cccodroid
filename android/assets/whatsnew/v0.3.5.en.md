## New: switch model and thinking effort right under the input bar

Tap the model name in the row under the input bar (now shown as "model · effort") and a panel slides up: thinking-effort buttons on top, then a search box, recently used models and the full list. With providers like OpenRouter that offer dozens of models, a couple of letters finds the one you want. Changes apply from your next message. "Switch model" in the + menu opens the same panel.

## New: "Seeker" look

Settings → Appearance now has "Seeker · Light / Dark / Follow system"; the original look is called "Native". Seeker uses the cool grey and blue of the DeepSeek Harness interface, with a light sweep across the title while Claude is thinking, a spinner on running tools and a pulsing blue dot while waiting for a reply. The status bar, dialogs and built-in browser change with it.
