## Fix: the page froze; you could scroll but not tap anything

A user hit this: the conversation still scrolled, but every button, the input box and even the snowflake in the top bar stopped responding, and only killing the app helped.

The cause: when the page shows a confirmation (switching to "Allow all", deleting a session, installing a component) and the split screen is resized, the screen rotates or the app goes to the background at that moment, the system can take the dialog away while the page keeps waiting for OK or Cancel. Now a dialog that disappears for any reason counts as Cancel.

There's also a safety net: if you tap and the page doesn't respond within 6 seconds, the app asks whether to reload it. A reply in progress keeps going.
