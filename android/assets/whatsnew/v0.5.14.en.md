This release is all about security: anything that can change your phone now needs a tap from you in cccodroid first.

## Things you now confirm

- **Privileged commands**: commands the AI runs through Shizuku ask you first, except read-only ones (system info, listing apps, reading logs and the like). The dialog shows the full command and who asked for it, and its buttons only become tappable after a second so you can't hit them by accident. Simple commands offer "Don't ask again"; ones with paths or environment variables can only be allowed one at a time.
- **Device tools**: turning on the device tools switch, or setting screen reading to automatic or always on, asks for confirmation.
- **Clipboard**: the AI needs your OK every time it reads the clipboard.
- **Claude Code downgrades**: if switching channels or pinning a version would install something older than what you have, you're asked first. Say no and nothing changes, and background auto-update won't sneak it in later.
- **Previewed pages**: offline previews can't reach the internet by default. When a page tries to connect out you get a notice, and it only goes through after you tap "Online". Tapping "Open in browser" also warns you first, since the browser doesn't have this protection.

## Other

- Offline devkit install now works by choosing "Choose a local file…" on first launch, and the file is checked against the published releases before installing.
- Archives with entries that would land outside the target folder are not extracted at all.
- Messages over 2 million characters now tell you to send them as a file instead.
- Buttons on component cards in Extensions no longer overflow.
- Endpoints on local http addresses can fetch the model list.
