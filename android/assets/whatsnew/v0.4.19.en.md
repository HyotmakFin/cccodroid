This release is about subagents: you can now see every subagent Claude sends out, and who contacted whom and why. It also fixes running `claude` directly in the terminal.

## Subagent card

- **Outside the steps**: when Claude launches subagents, a "Subagents" card appears below that group of steps, so you no longer have to dig through the steps to find them. Each subagent gets one row with its name, status, step count and time; running ones show what they're doing right now, finished ones show the first line of their report. Subagents launched by a subagent are indented below it.
- **Tap a row to see its work**: the steps of that subagent open up. Long runs show the latest 50 steps first, with "N earlier steps" to load the rest, so even big jobs open quickly.
- **Message log**: messages between subagents, and between a subagent and the main conversation, are listed at the bottom of the card as "who → whom · what about". Only the latest 3 show by default, and undelivered ones are marked. When a subagent wakes the main conversation, that reply starts with a line like "← who: what about".

## Briefs and reports saved as files

- Each subagent's brief and final report are saved under `.cccodroid/agents/` in the project folder; tap "Brief" or "Report" on the card to open them in the viewer. Long messages are saved too, leaving just a one-line reason in the message.
- When a batch of subagents has finished, a summary file is written, and Claude is told where it is along with your next message. Claude reads these files when it needs details instead of hitting permission prompts in `~/.claude`, and can still find them after the context is compacted.
- The folder comes with its own `.gitignore`, so it stays out of your repository.

## Other improvements

- **Reply timer**: the timer at the top right of a reply is hidden while Claude is writing and shows the total time once it's done, instead of sitting at 0s.

## Fixes

- **`claude` in the terminal failed with 401**: the terminal didn't get the provider and key set up in the app and fell back to an old key. Now `claude` in the terminal uses the provider selected when the terminal was opened; after switching providers in the app, open a new terminal tab.
