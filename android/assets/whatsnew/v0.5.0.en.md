The first 0.5.x release. No new features — just smoothing out a few rough edges people found in 0.4.20. Thanks to chenshi723 for tracking down all four, frame by frame.

## Fixes

- **Construct theme: rounded bits at the top and bottom of the sidebar edge**: the line between the docked sidebar and the chat is now one straight ink rule. The light paper is also a notch less yellow; the ink lines, red and hard shadows are unchanged. (#42)
- **Top bar title didn't match the sidebar**: for archived sessions the sidebar showed the summary title, but the top bar fell back to the first prompt. Both now agree — your own name first, then the archive title, then the first prompt — and search results follow the same rule. (#44)
- **Top bar and docked sidebar jumped when switching pages**: the subtitle was cleared before the new one loaded, so the top bar briefly got shorter. The subtitle now always keeps its line, and Help and Changelog have the same top bar height as other pages. (#43)
- **Overlapping text when closing Settings on wide screens**: the settings panel now fades out quickly before the backdrop does, so you no longer see both layers of text at once. (#41)
