Different conversations can now use different models and different providers, running side by side. In portrait you can also swipe the sidebar in and out.

## Each conversation can have its own model and provider

- The model, effort and provider in Settings are now **defaults**. A conversation you haven't set separately follows them, and changes along with them.
- To give one conversation a different model, tap the model label under the input box and pick one in the panel. Only that conversation changes, starting with the next message. "Back to following the default" sits at the top of the panel.
- The panel has a new "Provider" row (providers with a key, account login, or on-device). Tapping another provider only shows its models; the conversation switches when you pick a model.
- Switching providers mid-conversation asks first: the new provider's model re-reads the whole conversation, the prompt cache starts over, and billing follows the new provider.
- Several conversations can run at once — say one on your Claude account and one on DeepSeek — without getting in each other's way.
- When a conversation isn't on the default provider, the model label shows the provider name, and the quota / balance in the top bar follows the current conversation's provider.
- If an inherited effort level isn't supported by that provider (DeepSeek has no medium, for example), the nearest supported level is used, and the label shows the level actually in use.
- A brand-new conversation can be set up before its first message; the choice goes along with it.
- `/model` and `/effort` typed in a conversation change only that conversation; typed in a new, empty one they change the defaults.
- Projects and scheduled tasks can pick a provider too, and the model list follows it. New conversations in a project show the project's provider and model on the label.

## Swipe the sidebar in and out in portrait

- Swipe left to right in the chat area and the sidebar follows your finger; with it open, swipe right to left to put it away. A short swipe or a light flick is enough.
- Vertical scrolling works as before, and it won't trigger in the input box, the terminal, or on code blocks and tables that can still scroll sideways.
- The very left edge is still the system back gesture — start a little inside the chat area.
