The main change in this release: your account quota and provider balance are now visible at a glance instead of buried in Settings. There's also a new look called Zhuyue, and three bugs reported by users are fixed.

## Quota and balance at a glance

- The thin context bar under the top bar now ends with what's left on the current provider: the share of the 5-hour and weekly limits used when you sign in with a Claude account, the balance for DeepSeek, Kimi and similar, the plan windows for Zhipu, or today's tokens when nothing else is available. The text changes color as you run low.
- Tap the bar for details: two progress bars with reset times, or the balance, today's and total spending, and token usage. The card can refresh and links to the Usage page.
- The Usage page opens with a new **Quota and spending** card: full details for the current provider, plus one row per other provider you've added a key for, each expandable. The key card in Settings now shows just a one-line balance.
- Reminders: you're told once at 80% and once at 95% of a limit, reset each period, and you can set a balance threshold that reminds you once a day. Both switches are at the bottom of the card.
- No extra calls to providers: account quota already comes back with every turn, and balances use the gateway's cache with automatic re-checks after a turn. When the app opens, the last known numbers appear right away alongside the context figure.

## New look: Zhuyue

Settings → General → Appearance now offers Zhuyue, in light, dark or follow system. It's mostly black and white: the light version has a white background, light-gray sidebar and pages and white cards; the dark version is near-black. Buttons and selections are black (white in dark mode), and blue appears only on links, check marks and list numbers. The input box is a full pill and your messages are borderless with large rounded corners.

## Context bar colors

When the context is nearly or completely full, the bar no longer turns solid yellow or red. It fades from the theme's own color into a warm tone and then a red, each tuned to the light and dark version of every look.

## Fixes

- **Copying a code block failed and showed an interface error**: the system sometimes refuses to let the page write to the clipboard, and that refusal surfaced in the top bar. Copying now goes straight to the system clipboard, everywhere: replies, the terminal, the group number and more.
- **The Copy button scrolled away with the code**: it now stays in the top-right corner of the block.
- **Links often opened a blank browser pane until you dragged its edge**: the page reported where the pane goes before asking the app to create it, and that position was lost. The app now remembers it and applies it once the pane exists.
